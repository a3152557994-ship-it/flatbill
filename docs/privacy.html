Privacy Policy — FlatBill

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

FlatBill ("we", "our", or "the App") is built with a strict offline-first, zero-tracking architecture. Your business records, client contact details, invoices, estimates, signatures, and job-site photos belong exclusively to you.

1. Zero Cloud Data Collection

FlatBill does not require account registration and does not transmit or store your invoices, estimates, client lists, photos, or signatures on any developer servers. All application data is stored locally on your device.

2. Camera & Photo Library Access

If you choose to upload a business logo or attach before/after job-site photos to an invoice or estimate, FlatBill accesses your camera or selected photos solely to embed them into your locally generated PDF documents.

3. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Purchases and subscriptions are processed directly by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit 2). We never receive or store your credit card or payment credentials.

4. Analytics & Advertising

FlatBill contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, zero tracking pixels, and zero behavioral analytics.

5. Data Export & Deletion

You can export all your records at any time as JSON or CSV from Business & Settings, or delete all local data by removing the app from your device.
