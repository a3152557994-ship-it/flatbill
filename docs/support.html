Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my Lifetime Pro purchase on a new iPhone?
Open FlatBill → Business & Settings → tap "Restore Purchases" while signed into the same Apple Account.

How do I back up my invoices and clients?
Go to Business & Settings → tap "Export Full Local Backup (JSON)" to save your entire ledger to iCloud Drive, Files, or email.

Does FlatBill take a cut of my client payments?
No. FlatBill charges 0% transaction fees. Put your Zelle, Venmo, Cash App, Check, or Bank instructions directly on your PDF.
